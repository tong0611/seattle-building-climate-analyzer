# Data-quality layer: results and evaluation

_Generated by `beps-quality-eval`. Flags mean "worth a look", not wrongdoing._

## How the trust score works

Each building-year starts at 100. Each **critical** flag subtracts 60; each **warning** subtracts 10-25 depending on its impact (see Rules); the Isolation Forest anomaly model subtracts 25 (top 1% most unusual) or 10 (top 3%). Tiers: **high** >= 80, **medium** 50-79, **low** < 50. Rows with any critical flag are excluded from peer modeling.

## Summary

- Trust tiers: high **34,220**, medium **1,004**, low **3,085** (of 38,309 building-years)
- Usable for modeling: **35,362** rows
- Review workload: **7.8%** of rows the city marks 'No Issue' are surfaced (below high trust)
- Of 126 rows the city flags for possible reporting errors, 83.3% are surfaced, but mostly because their metrics are missing; among the 24 that have values, **3** are surfaced (see caveat below)

## Evaluation: injected reporting errors

Realistic errors were injected into clean records (city 'No Issue', no flags, not anomalous), with derived fields (site energy, EUI, GHG) recomputed consistently so the error is not trivially visible. The full pipeline, including the anomaly model, was then re-run. `surfaced_pct` = share of corrupted rows that drop below high trust.

| error_type | description | n_injected | caught_by_rules_pct | caught_by_model_pct | surfaced_pct | surfaced_without_model_pct | unusable_for_modeling_pct |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| floor_area_x10 | Floor area typed 10x too large | 300 | 100 | 99.7 | 99.3 | 99.3 | 67.7 |
| floor_area_x0.1 | Floor area typed 10x too small | 300 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 9 |
| electricity_x1000 | Electricity in wrong units (Wh as kWh) | 300 | 100 | 98.3 | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| electricity_x0.001 | Electricity in wrong units (MWh as kWh) | 300 | 99.7 | 85.7 | 99.7 | 99.7 | 85 |
| electricity_x2 | Electricity meter double-counted | 300 | 87.3 | 54 | 87.3 | 87.3 | 0.3 |
| gas_meter_missing | Gas meter left out (gas >= 20% of energy) | 300 | 84.7 | 20.7 | 84.7 | 84.7 | 6.3 |

`surfaced_without_model_pct` is an ablation: the same score with the anomaly model removed. On these known error types the rules do all of the work; the model is kept, at low weight, as a catch-all for inconsistency patterns no single rule covers.
