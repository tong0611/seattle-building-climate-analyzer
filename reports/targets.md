# Target gaps and estimated penalty exposure

_Generated by `beps-targets`. **All dollar figures are estimates** of exposure if each building's latest trustworthy performance continued unchanged to its deadline. They are not predictions that penalties will be issued._

## Method

- Basis: each building's most recent year with no critical data-quality flag, 2023 or later (3,659 buildings).
- **BEPS GHGI** recomputed as weather-normalized fuel use x the 2031-2035 provisional emission factors (electricity 0.0029, gas 0.053, steam 0.081 kg/kBtu) / the floor area the energy was reported for (excluding parking). Targets are floor-area-weighted across uses using the Director's Rule Appendix A mapping.
- **BEPS penalty** = $10/sf nonresidential or $7.50/sf multifamily, once per 5-year interval. **Alternative compliance payment (ACP)** = max($1,250 or $2,500, excess tCO2e/yr x 5 x $190), capped at the penalty. The ACP is the cheaper path, so it is the more realistic cost of non-performance.
- **CBPS** applies EUI targets to Tier 1 buildings (> 50,000 sf of nonresidential, hotel, or dormitory space). Maximum penalty $5,000 + $1.50/sf. Owners can also comply through a 15% EUI reduction or investment-criteria pathway, which this estimate cannot see.

Known limits: affordable housing ($2.50/sf and a possible 2031-35 extension) cannot be identified in the data, so multifamily estimates may be high; end-use deductions, campus/portfolio compliance, and CBPS target adjustments are not modeled; per-fuel weather normalization is approximated.
