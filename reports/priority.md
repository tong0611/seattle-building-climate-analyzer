# Priority ranking

_Generated by `beps-priority`. Scores rank where attention is likely to matter most; they do not judge owners. Exposure figures are estimates._

## Score

`score = 100 x confidence x (0.5 x impact + 0.3 x urgency + 0.2 x opportunity)`

| component | meaning |
|---|---|
| impact | excess emissions above the 2031-35 BEPS target (or excess energy above the CBPS target), percentile among over-target buildings |
| urgency | deadline proximity: BEPS 2031 = 1.0 ... 2035 = 0.2; CBPS 2026 = 1.0, 2027 = 0.9, 2028 = 0.8 |
| opportunity | EUI above peers (actual / expected - 1, capped at 1) x peer confidence |
| confidence | data-quality trust score / 100 |

- Over-target buildings need a median **40%** emissions cut; gas is a median **96%** of their 2031 emissions.
- **252** over-target buildings could reach the target by matching their peers' energy efficiency alone (same fuel mix); the rest need fuel switching.

- Versus simply ranking by excess emissions, 20 of the top 50 are the same; the score adds deadline urgency, efficiency opportunity, and data confidence.
