# Peer model: expected energy and emissions intensity

_Generated by `beps-peer`. Gaps are estimates and mean "worth a look"._

## Setup

- Targets: weather-normalized site EUI (kBtu/sf) and GHG intensity (kgCO2e/sf), modeled on the log scale.
- Training rows: 35,362 building-years with no critical data-quality flag.
- Validation: 5-fold GroupKFold **by building**; every row is predicted by a model that never saw that building in any year.
- Features: floor-area share by use type, size, parking ratio, floors, number of buildings, building type, data year. No fuel mix and no building age (both are levers, not peer traits).

## Accuracy on held-out buildings

`typical_error_pct` = median absolute error expressed as a percentage. Remaining error is expected: what the model cannot explain from physical characteristics (operations, equipment, fuel choice) is the performance gap this tool looks for.

## Does the gap measure real performance?

- **ENERGY STAR agreement:** Spearman correlation of the EUI gap with EPA's ENERGY STAR score (negative = agrees). ENERGY STAR also adjusts for operating hours, workers, and other inputs not in this dataset, so perfect agreement is not expected.
- **Persistence:** correlation of a building's gap with its gap the next year. Noise would not persist.

| model | spearman_vs_energy_star | spearman_vs_energy_star_within_type | gap_persistence_next_year |
|---|---|---|---|
| use-type median | -0.54 | -0.67 | 0.89 |
| blended use median | -0.54 | -0.68 | 0.89 |
| gradient boosting | -0.55 | -0.65 | 0.88 |
| blended median + boosting | -0.55 | -0.66 | 0.88 |
